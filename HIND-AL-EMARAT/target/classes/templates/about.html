Our Fragrant Heritage

Hind Al Emarat represents the zenith of luxury perfumery, creating a bridge between the ancient forest agarwoods of Assam, India and the magnificent spice souks of the Arabian Gulf.

The Master's Vision

Established with the goal of preserving traditional distillation processes, Hind Al Emarat sources raw ingredients directly from local tribal harvesters in Kashmir, Assam, and Kannauj.

Each batch of Rose oil (Gulab) or White Musk concentrate takes up to three months of delicate filtering inside copper and gold vessels. We avoid industrial chemical acceleration to ensure the botanical spirit of the perfume is fully captured.

Our name represents the friendship and historical trade ties between the perfume capital of India (Kannauj) and the luxury perfume markets of the United Arab Emirates.

Core Principles

Sovereignty of Oud

We use 100% natural, legally sourced agarwood oils, valuing the rare trees that have aged for over 50 years in northeast Indian forest reserves.

Kannauj Attar Pride

We support traditional hydro-distillation workers in Kannauj, keeping alive a 400-year-old botanical distillation legacy.

Uncompromising Sillage

Our products are formulated at maximum Eau de Parfum concentrations or absolute attar states, ensuring legendary projection and longevity.
